About Garage2Home

Garage2Home (G2H) is a Bareilly startup bringing mobility to garage services, so the garage comes to you.

01 Who we are

G2H is a startup based out of Bareilly bringing mobility to garage services.

02 What we do

We bring essential garage services to the consumer's doorstep: services that can be performed at home with complete peace of mind.

03 The problem we solve

We bring mobility and convenience to consumers, creating an environment where they can make the most of their time and focus on what matters to them.

04 What is innovative

We use technology to streamline consumer touch points and intake through an internally developed system. We innovate with a mix of technology, creativity and convenience.

05 How we are different

We drive the garage to your doorstep, not the usual way around.

06 The basis of our innovation

The reliability of an authorized service center is compromised by high inconvenience and selective servicing, while the local market offers cost-effective solutions with a compromise on quality, warranty and service experience. We bridge that gap and offer a realistic solution to the consumer.
